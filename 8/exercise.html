The Hobbit: Or There and Back Again The Unexpected Party The story begins with Bilbo Baggins, a quiet and respectable hobbit who loves his comfortable hole in the ground and his six meals a day. His life is turned upside down when the wizard Gandalf arrives with thirteen dwarves, led by Thorin Oakenshield. They are on a quest to reclaim their ancestral home, the Lonely Mountain, and its vast treasure from the fearsome dragon, Smaug. Much to his own surprise, Bilbo is hired as their "burglar."
