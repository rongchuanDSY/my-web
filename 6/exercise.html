The Art of Liquid Design

This layout demonstrates a liquid design approach. Instead of fixed pixel widths, the layout uses percentage-based columns. As the browser window expands or shrinks, the layout adapts smoothly while maintaining readable content widths.

The container also uses a minimum and maximum width to prevent the design from stretching too far on large monitors or becoming cramped on smaller screens. This technique is commonly used in responsive web design to maintain usability.

Typography combines Playfair Display for headings with Lato for body text, creating a balance between elegance and readability.
